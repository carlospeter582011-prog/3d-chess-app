# Recorded sound effects (optional)

By default the app synthesises its sounds. To use recorded ones instead, put files here named after the sound:

`move`, `capture`, `check`, `end` with extension `.wav`, `.ogg` or `.mp3` (e.g. `move.wav`).

Only use files you have the right to ship (public domain / CC0, or licensed for commercial use) and keep the
credit in this file. Short (< 1 s), mono or stereo, any sample rate.

`checkmate.mp3` and `stalemate.mp3` are the end-of-game jingles (played on checkmate / stalemate). They were supplied by the
app owner; make sure you hold the rights to ship them (the stalemate clip is a Team Fortress 2 sound).
